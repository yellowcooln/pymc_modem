# Dormant dual RAK13302 policy (not a supported RF configuration)

`firmware/include/rak13302_frontend_policy.h` defines two independent ANT_SW controllers and one cooperative exclusive-TX arbiter. It is included for compilation on `ethermesh_1w`, but **no instance is constructed in firmware**, no GPIO is configured by this policy, and the existing E22P primary radio is unchanged. There is no Duo listener, secondary TX, or RF enablement. The two proposed ANT_SW GPIOs (27 and 19) are test fixtures, **not verified P4 carrier wiring**; GPIO27 is currently E22P RF_EN on `ethermesh_1w`, so it cannot be assigned to a RAK13302 in the current build.

On the RAK3401 reference, RAK13302 ANT_SW (module pin 31) drives tied SKY66122 CSD/CPS: HIGH in RX and TX, while SX1262 DIO2/CTX selects the path; DIO3 supplies the 1.8 V TCXO. This is not E22P power-enable behavior. Each controller latches ANT_SW LOW before output mode and requires an explicit externally established supply-ready condition before HIGH. The supply-ready input is a **caller assertion**, not a rail sensor or permission to assert a rail. Normal `shutdown()` rejects active TX without touching GPIO or releasing the grant. On unexpected supply withdrawal during TX, the controller faults, retains ANT_SW and the grant, and rejects new TX; this software policy cannot restore a lost physical rail. **Only after RadioLib `finishTransmit()` or `standby()` succeeds and the SX1262 can no longer transmit** may the caller invoke the policy's `finishTransmit()` acknowledgement. With supply ready it transitions to RX with ANT_SW still HIGH; after withdrawal it drives LOW before releasing the grant. If that LOW write fails, it retains the grant in Fault; after radio-stop acknowledgement, retry `shutdown()` to drive LOW and release. Never call the acknowledgement on a failed radio stop; neither policy method stops the radio. GPIO adapters must report write failures accurately. Arbiter acquisition/release is private to controllers; it admits one TX at a time in a serialized firmware context, not from concurrent interrupts.

**Do not connect this to live paths until hardware is present:** confirm the exact carrier variant, rail/boost wiring and settling, ANT_SW pin ownership and safe default, radio SPI/IRQ/TCXO connections, DIO2 switching, power and thermal limits. Exclusive TX does **not** protect an adjacent receiving front end from overload. A separate hardware-validated policy to hold or isolate the other receiver during local TX is required before Duo RF operation, along with failure recovery and bench RF tests.

Host test: `g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -Ifirmware/include firmware/tests/rak13302_frontend_policy_test.cpp -o /path/to/test && /path/to/test`. This exercises two recording GPIO instances, per-pin isolation, supply gate, initial LOW, shutdown and supply loss during TX, explicit stop acknowledgement, HIGH retention into RX, TX denial/release, failed LOW retry, invalid IDs/pins, failed configuration and failed writes. It does not validate physical hardware.
